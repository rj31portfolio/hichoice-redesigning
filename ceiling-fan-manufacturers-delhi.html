How do I choose a ceiling fan for my room?+

Start with the room dimensions, ceiling height and installation position. Then compare the available blade sweep, air delivery, finish and motor options. Share your room details with Hi Choice to discuss suitable models.

Where can I get model specifications and prices?+

Enquire using the button on any product card. Our team can confirm the current specifications, available finishes, price and stock for that model before you order.

Can dealers and project buyers enquire about bulk orders?+

Yes. Share the models you are interested in, quantities and delivery location. The Hi Choice team can discuss availability, order terms and a quotation for your requirement.

What should I check when comparing energy use?+

Ask for the selected model's rated power consumption, motor type and applicable energy label. Compare these alongside air delivery so you can assess running costs and comfort together.

Do you also offer other types of fans?+

The catalogue includes ventilation, pedestal, table and wall, and all-purpose fans, along with Madhani products. Use the category filters to explore each range.
