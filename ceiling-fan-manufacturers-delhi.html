Why Energy Efficiency Matters So Much Now

Since 2023, the government has made BEE star ratings mandatory for all ceiling fans. This rule became even stricter in 2026. Old fans that used 75–80 watts are being replaced by better options.

The biggest change is the shift to BLDC technology. As a BLDC fan manufacturer, we see why more people and businesses are choosing these fans. BLDC motors use magnets instead of old brushes. This reduces friction, removes humming noise, and cuts electricity use by up to 65%.

A good 5 star ceiling fan manufacturer now makes fans that run on just 28 to 35 watts while still giving strong airflow. Over time, this saves a lot of money — especially in hotels, offices, or housing projects that use hundreds of fans.

How to Choose the Right Ceiling Fan Manufacturer

Here is a simple checklist to help you decide:

  • Check real experience — Companies with 30–40+ years usually have better systems and consistency.
  • Look at energy ratings — Prefer BEE certified ceiling fan manufacturer options, especially 4-star and 5-star models.
  • Ask about motor type — BLDC motors save the most electricity and run quietly.
  • Check air delivery (CMM) — Speed (RPM) alone is not enough. A good high speed ceiling fan manufacturer should clearly mention Cubic Meters per Minute (CMM).
  • See the designs — If you need beautiful fans, choose a strong decorative ceiling fan manufacturer or designer ceiling fan manufacturer.
  • Confirm after-sales support — Good manufacturers offer proper warranty and service network across India.
  • For business buyers — Ask if they can work as a ceiling fan OEM manufacturer or do private labelling with your brand name.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a ceiling fan BEE 5-star certified in 2026?

A fan needs a high “Service Value”. This means it delivers good airflow (CMM) while using very little electricity. Most 5-star fans today use BLDC motors to meet this standard.

Can I put my own brand name on the fans?

Yes. If you work with a ceiling fan OEM manufacturer or private label ceiling fan manufacturer, they will make the fans exactly as you want and print your brand name and logo.

Why should I buy from manufacturers in Delhi NCR?

Are BLDC fans worth the higher price?

Yes, for most homes and businesses. A good BLDC fan manufacturer model can save thousands of rupees in electricity over 5–7 years and runs much quieter.

Do decorative fans also come in energy-saving versions?

Yes. Many decorative ceiling fan manufacturer and designer ceiling fan manufacturer options now offer BLDC motors with good BEE ratings.
