How to Choose the Right Electric Iron for Your Customers

Many wholesalers ask us this question. Here is simple advice:

  • For normal home use — 750W or 1000W dry iron works well. It is light and uses less electricity.
  • For families with heavy clothes or curtains — Go for 1200W or 1600W steam iron.
  • For shops and commercial use — Heavy weight plancha iron gives better results.
  • For easy daily use — Lightweight iron with non-stick plate is a good choice.

Always check that the iron has temperature control and auto cut-off. These two features make the iron safer and last longer.
