Our Range of Electric Irons+

We make different types of irons so you can pick what sells best in your area.

Dry Iron

Our dry irons are light and strong. They work well for everyday clothes. Popular models come in 750W, 1000W, and 1200W. They have non-stick plates so clothes do not stick. Temperature control helps you iron different fabrics safely.

Steam Iron

Need more power? Our steam irons give good steam for tough wrinkles. The 1600W models are great for heavy clothes and curtains. They heat fast and save time.

Heavy Weight Iron (Plancha Iron)

Many shopkeepers still like traditional heavy weight irons. Our plancha irons are strong and give good pressure. They are popular in markets across North India.

Lightweight Iron

For homes that want easy handling, we have lightweight models. Women and older people find them comfortable to use.

All our irons have auto cut-off for safety and strong cords that last long.

How to Choose the Right Electric Iron for Your Customers+

Many wholesalers ask us this question. Here is simple advice:

  • For normal home use — 750W or 1000W dry iron works well. It is light and uses less electricity.
  • For families with heavy clothes or curtains — Go for 1200W or 1600W steam iron.
  • For shops and commercial use — Heavy weight plancha iron gives better results.
  • For easy daily use — Lightweight iron with non-stick plate is a good choice.

Always check that the iron has temperature control and auto cut-off. These two features make the iron safer and last longer.
